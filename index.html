Welcome to MINE MASTER

Heavy Quarry Operations, Ore Processing & Fleet Logistics

🏆 Your Goal

Turn a rugged open-pit quarry into a high-output mining empire! Dig deep through Dirt, Limestone, Granite, and purple Basalt to uncover rich seams of Iron, Copper, Gold, Lithium, and rare Meteorites & Crystal Geodes. Fulfill industrial contracts, upgrade hydraulics, and maximize profits.

🔄 How the Quarry Logistics Loop Works

1. 🚜 Excavator (Pit Face)

Breaks rock seams along the quarry wall and piles raw ore onto the pit floor. Upgrade Hydraulics to break purple Basalt!

2. 🏗️ Wheel Loader (Pit Load Zone)

Scoops loose ore piles and loads them into the Articulated Dumper (or directly into the Crusher).

3. 🚛 Articulated Dumper (Smart A* Haul)

Uses smart pathfinding to shuttle heavy 24t+ loads back and forth from the Pit Load Zone to the Crusher Plant.

4. ⚙️ Crusher & Export Semi-Truck

Refines raw rock into metal ingots, loads the Export Semi-Truck via crane, and sells for cash!

🎮 Intuitive Controls

  • Smart Auto-Shuttle: Your starter crew automatically navigates around obstacles and runs the pit-to-crusher loop!
  • Tap / Click to Command: Tap any machine to select it, then tap anywhere on the map to route it there automatically.
  • Manual Override: Drive any selected machine directly with WASD / Arrow Keys + Spacebar (or the on-screen Joystick + Action Button on iPad/iPhone).
